About cert/map, the cybersecurity certification roadmap

cert/map charts 272 cybersecurity certifications from 62 vendors across 16 domains and four seniority levels, from entry-level credentials such as CompTIA Security+ to expert ones such as OSCE3 and CISSP.

Each entry lists the current exam price in USD, exam length and format, renewal cycle and prerequisites, checked against the vendor's own page. Pick the certifications you are aiming for to get a path ordered by prerequisite with its total cost and study time, then share it with a link. The project is open source and the dataset is free to reuse.

Questions and answers

What is a cybersecurity certification roadmap?

A roadmap lays certifications out by subject and by seniority so you can see which ones lead to which. cert/map places 272 certifications from 62 vendors on a grid of 16 domains and four levels, from beginner to expert, and shows the price, exam format and prerequisites of each.

Which cybersecurity certification should a beginner start with?

The most widely recognised entry-level certifications on the roadmap are Security+ ($439), CC ($199), A+ ($274), ACE ($125), AZ-900 ($99). Which one fits depends on the direction you want to take: general security, networking, cloud or hands-on testing.

How much do cybersecurity certifications cost?

Exam prices on the roadmap run from free to $2,683. The cheapest paid exams are CRTA ($69), Vault Associate ($71), AZ-900 ($99), and the most expensive are BTL2 ($2,683), CSMIE ($1,997), OSCP+ ($1,749). Prices are list prices in US dollars for the exam, without training unless the vendor only sells them together.

Are there free cybersecurity certifications?

Yes. CTI 101, OSCE3 have no exam fee.

Which certifications have a hands-on exam?

80 of the 272 certifications are assessed with a practical exam in a lab rather than multiple choice, among them Cisco EH, MCRTA, OSCC-SEC, PJPT, CDSA, CKA.

Which certifications count for DoD 8140?

31 certifications on the roadmap are recognised under the US Department of Defense 8140 framework, including Network+, Security+, CCNA Cybersecurity, CEH, CHFI, CySA+.

How current are the prices?

237 of 272 prices carry the date they were last checked against the vendor's own page; the most recent check was on 2026-11-10. Each certification page shows its own date. Vendors change prices without notice, so confirm before paying.

Can I build and share my own certification path?

Yes. Select certifications in the interactive roadmap and it orders them by prerequisite and adds up exam cost and study time. Give the path a name and copy its link: anyone who opens it sees the same roadmap.

Data and licence

The whole dataset is one CSV file, also published as JSON, with a plain-text digest for language models at llms.txt and llms-full.txt. The code is MIT licensed and the dataset is CC BY 4.0. Corrections are welcome on GitHub.
