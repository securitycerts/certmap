Cisco certifications at a glance

How many Cisco certifications are there?

cert/map lists 7 Cisco certifications: 2 beginner, 2 intermediate, 2 advanced, 1 expert.

How much do Cisco certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $125 (CCST CS) to $1,600 (CCIE Security). 1 of the 7 have no published standalone price or are free.

Which Cisco certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and CCST CS (Cisco Certified Support Technician Cybersecurity) is the usual entry point there.

Do Cisco certifications expire?

6 of the 7 have a renewal cycle (for example CCST CS, every 5 years); the rest do not expire.

Are Cisco exams hands-on?

2 of the 7 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
