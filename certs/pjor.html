Practical OSINT Research Professional PORP

Practical OSINT Research Professional (PORP), formerly Practical Junior OSINT Researcher. Hands-on OSINT investigation certification with a practical exam and report.

PORP is a beginner-level OSINT certification from TCM Security. The exam costs $399. The exam runs for 3 days. It does not expire.
