Social Links OSINT Certification Program Level 1 OCP1

Vendor program on OSINT investigation with Social Links products in five modules (social media, messengers, niche platforms, advanced features, corporate sources), each followed by a test. The certificate is issued after all tests are passed and is valid for three years.

OCP1 is a beginner-level OSINT certification from Social Links. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It renews every 3 years.
