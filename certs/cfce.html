Certified Forensic Computer Examiner CFCE

Two-phase digital forensics certification: a peer review of four practical problems, then a hard drive practical and a 100-question knowledge test. External candidates must show 72 hours of relevant training.

CFCE is an advanced-level Forensics certification from IACIS. The exam costs $800. It renews every 3 years.
