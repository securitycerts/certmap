The difference between A+ and Security+

  • A+ is the cheaper exam: $274 against $439 for Security+, a difference of $165.
  • Both sit at beginner level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • Security+ is recognized under DoD 8140. A+ is not listed.
  • Both are issued by CompTIA.

What each one covers

A+. Entry-level IT support certification covering hardware and operating systems.

Security+. Foundational vendor-neutral cybersecurity certification covering core concepts and hands-on skills.
