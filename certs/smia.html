Certified Social Media Intelligence Analyst SMIA

Certification in social media intelligence and online investigations with an OSINT foundation module, assessed by a proctored board exam. Eligibility requires a degree or diploma plus relevant paid professional experience.

SMIA is an intermediate-level OSINT certification from McAfee Institute. The exam costs $1,397. It renews every 2 years.
