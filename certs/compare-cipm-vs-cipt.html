The difference between CIPM and CIPT

  • Both exams cost $550.
  • CIPT sits at intermediate level and CIPM at advanced level, so CIPT is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • Both renew every 2 years.
  • Both are issued by IAPP.

What each one covers

CIPM. Privacy program management certification.

CIPT. Privacy by design technology certification.
