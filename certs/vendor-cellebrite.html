Cellebrite certifications at a glance

How many Cellebrite certifications are there?

cert/map lists 4 Cellebrite certifications: 1 intermediate, 1 advanced, 2 expert.

How much do Cellebrite certifications cost?

Cellebrite does not publish standalone exam prices for the certifications listed here.

Which Cellebrite certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is intermediate, and CCO (Cellebrite Certified Operator for Inseyets) is the usual entry point there.

Do Cellebrite certifications expire?

3 of the 4 have a renewal cycle (for example CCO, every 2 years); the rest do not expire.

Are Cellebrite exams hands-on?

Yes. Every Cellebrite exam listed here is practical and performance-based.
