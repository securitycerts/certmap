CNCF certifications at a glance

How many CNCF certifications are there?

cert/map lists 3 CNCF certifications: 1 beginner, 1 intermediate, 1 advanced.

How much do CNCF certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $250 (KCSA) to $445 (CKS).

Which CNCF certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and KCSA (Kubernetes and Cloud Native Security Associate) is the usual entry point there.

Do CNCF certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example KCSA every 2 years.

Are CNCF exams hands-on?

2 of the 3 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
