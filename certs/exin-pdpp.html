EXIN Privacy & Data Protection Professional PDPP

Advanced-level GDPR certification on data protection policies, the roles of controller, processor and DPO, impact assessments and breach response. Requires accredited training with practical assignments and a two-hour exam of 40 multiple-choice questions with a 65% pass mark.

PDPP is an intermediate-level GRC certification from EXIN. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. The exam runs for 120 minutes. It does not expire.
