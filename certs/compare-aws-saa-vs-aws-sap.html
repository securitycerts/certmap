The difference between SAA-C03 and SAP-C03

  • SAA-C03 is the cheaper exam: $150 against $300 for SAP-C03, a difference of $150.
  • SAA-C03 sits at intermediate level and SAP-C03 at expert level, so SAA-C03 is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The SAP-C03 exam is longer: 180 minutes against 130 minutes for SAA-C03.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • SAA-C03 is a prerequisite of SAP-C03.
  • Both are issued by AWS.

What each one covers

SAA-C03. AWS architecture certification covering core services and design.

SAP-C03. Advanced AWS architecture and design certification.
