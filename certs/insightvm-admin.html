Vendor certification for administrators of the Rapid7 InsightVM vulnerability management product. Online open-book exam of two hours with a passing score of 80%, awarded as a digital badge.

InsightVM Admin is an intermediate-level Security Operations certification from Rapid7. The exam costs $215. The exam runs for 120 minutes. It does not expire.
