The difference between CGRC and CRISC

  • CRISC is the cheaper exam: $575 against $599 for CGRC, a difference of $24.
  • CGRC sits at advanced level and CRISC at expert level, so CGRC is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CRISC exam is longer: 240 minutes against 180 minutes for CGRC.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • CGRC is recognized under DoD 8140. CRISC is not listed.

What each one covers

CGRC. Risk management framework certification for GRC professionals.

CRISC. Enterprise risk management certification for risk and control professionals.
