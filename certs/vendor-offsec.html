OffSec certifications at a glance

How many OffSec certifications are there?

cert/map lists 16 OffSec certifications: 3 beginner, 5 intermediate, 2 advanced, 6 expert.

How much do OffSec certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $899 (OSCC-SEC) to $1,749 (OSWE). 5 of the 16 have no published standalone price or are free.

Which OffSec certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and KLCP (Kali Linux Certified Professional) is the usual entry point there.

Do OffSec certifications expire?

8 of the 16 have a renewal cycle (for example OSCC-SEC, every 3 years); the rest do not expire.

Are OffSec exams hands-on?

8 of the 16 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
