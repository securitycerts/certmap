Microsoft certifications at a glance

How many Microsoft certifications are there?

cert/map lists 10 Microsoft certifications: 2 beginner, 7 intermediate, 1 expert.

How much do Microsoft certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $99 (AZ-900) to $165 (SC-100). 1 of the 10 have no published standalone price or are free.

Which Microsoft certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and AZ-900 (Microsoft Azure Fundamentals) is the usual entry point there.

Do Microsoft certifications expire?

8 of the 10 have a renewal cycle (for example AZ-104, every 1 year); the rest do not expire.

Are Microsoft exams hands-on?

The Microsoft exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
