The difference between A+ and Network+

  • A+ is the cheaper exam: $274 against $399 for Network+, a difference of $125.
  • Both sit at beginner level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • Network+ is recognized under DoD 8140. A+ is not listed.
  • Both are issued by CompTIA.

What each one covers

A+. Entry-level IT support certification covering hardware and operating systems.

Network+. Foundational networking certification covering protocols, topologies and basic security.
