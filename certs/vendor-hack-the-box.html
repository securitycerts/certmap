Hack The Box certifications at a glance

How many Hack The Box certifications are there?

cert/map lists 9 Hack The Box certifications: 1 beginner, 2 intermediate, 2 advanced, 4 expert.

How much do Hack The Box certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $105 (CJCA) to $450 (CDEP).

Which Hack The Box certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and CJCA (HTB Certified Junior Cybersecurity Associate) is the usual entry point there.

Do Hack The Box certifications expire?

None of the Hack The Box certifications listed here expires.

Are Hack The Box exams hands-on?

Yes. Every Hack The Box exam listed here is practical and performance-based.
