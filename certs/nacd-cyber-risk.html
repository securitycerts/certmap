CERT Certificate in Cybersecurity Oversight (NACD Cyber-Risk Oversight Program) NACD CERT

Online self-paced programme of about 16 hours for board directors on overseeing cyber risk, with a cyber-crisis simulation. Each module ends with an exam that needs 80% to pass, all within one year of registration; the certificate does not expire.

NACD CERT is an advanced-level Security & Risk Management certification from NACD. The exam costs $4,495. It does not expire.
