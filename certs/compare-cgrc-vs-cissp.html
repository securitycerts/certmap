Where they differ

  • CGRC is the cheaper exam: $599 against $749 for CISSP, a difference of $150.
  • CGRC sits at advanced level and CISSP at expert level, so CGRC is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • Both are issued by ISC2.

What each one covers

CGRC. Risk management framework certification for GRC professionals.

CISSP. Gold-standard cybersecurity certification covering 8 domains; requires 5 years experience.
