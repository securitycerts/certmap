The difference between eJPT and CEH

  • eJPT is the cheaper exam: $250 against $1,199 for CEH, a difference of $949.
  • eJPT sits at beginner level and CEH at intermediate level, so eJPT is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The eJPT exam is longer: 2 days against 240 minutes for CEH.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • CEH is recognized under DoD 8140. eJPT is not listed.

What each one covers

eJPT. Entry-level practical penetration testing certification.

CEH. Broad ethical hacking certification covering attack methodology.
