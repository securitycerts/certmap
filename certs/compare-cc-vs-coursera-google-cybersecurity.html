Where they differ

  • CC is the cheaper exam: $199 against $294 for Google Cyber, a difference of $95.
  • Both sit at beginner level on the roadmap.
  • Google Cyber is assessed with a practical exam. CC is a written or multiple-choice exam.
  • CC: renews every 3 years. Google Cyber: does not expire.

What each one covers

CC. Entry-level cybersecurity certification covering foundational security principles; no work experience required.

Google Cyber. Nine-course entry-level program covering security frameworks, network security, Linux, SQL, SIEM and IDS tools, incident detection and response, and Python automation. Course-based certificate assessed through quizzes, hands-on labs and portfolio activities, with no proctored exam.
