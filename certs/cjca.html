Entry-level practical exam combining a gray-box penetration test with validation of SIEM alerts, followed by a written report. Requires completing the Junior Cybersecurity Analyst job-role path.

CJCA is a beginner-level Security & Risk Management certification from Hack The Box. The exam costs $105. The exam runs for 5 days. It does not expire.
