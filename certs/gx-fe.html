GIAC Experienced Forensics Examiner GX-FE

Applied Knowledge exam of 25 CyberLive hands-on challenges in four hours, open book, covering application execution evidence, deleted artifact recovery, cloud storage, browser and USB activity and account usage on Windows systems. Counts toward the GSE portfolio.

GX-FE is an expert-level Forensics certification from GIAC. The exam costs $1,299. The exam runs for 240 minutes. It renews every 4 years.
