LPI certifications at a glance

How many LPI certifications are there?

cert/map lists 4 LPI certifications: 2 beginner, 1 intermediate, 1 advanced.

How much do LPI certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $120 (LPI SE) to $200 (LPIC-3 303).

Which LPI certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and LPI SE (LPI Security Essentials) is the usual entry point there.

Do LPI certifications expire?

3 of the 4 have a renewal cycle (for example LPIC-1, every 5 years); the rest do not expire.

Are LPI exams hands-on?

The LPI exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
