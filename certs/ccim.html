CREST Certified Incident Manager CCIM

Certified-level exam for incident response managers with a multiple-choice component and a written component of long-form and scenario questions, covering containment, evidence handling, communications, recovery and legal issues. Delivered in two parts at Pearson VUE, with part 2 taken within three months of part 1.

CCIM is an expert-level Incident Handling certification from CREST. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It does not expire.
