Certified Cloud Native Security Expert CCNSE

Practical exam of five challenges in a six-hour window, followed by 24 hours to submit a report, covering container and Kubernetes security. Lifetime credential.

CCNSE is an advanced-level Cloud/SysOps certification from Practical DevSecOps. The exam costs $999. The exam runs for 360 minutes. It does not expire.
