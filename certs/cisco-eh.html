Cisco Certificate in Ethical Hacking Cisco EH

Certificate earned by completing the Cisco Networking Academy Ethical Hacker course and one capture the flag challenge in Cisco U. It has no traditional exam and is valid for life.

Cisco EH is a beginner-level Penetration Testing certification from Cisco. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It does not expire.
