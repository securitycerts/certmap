CREST certifications at a glance

How many CREST certifications are there?

cert/map lists 10 CREST certifications: 2 beginner, 2 intermediate, 1 advanced, 5 expert.

How much do CREST certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $369 (CPIA) to $805 (CRTIA). 5 of the 10 have no published standalone price or are free.

Which CREST certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and CPIA (CREST Practitioner Intrusion Analyst) is the usual entry point there.

Do CREST certifications expire?

2 of the 10 have a renewal cycle (for example CRIA, every 3 years); the rest do not expire.

Are CREST exams hands-on?

4 of the 10 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
