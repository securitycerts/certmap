CREST certifications at a glance

How many CREST certifications are there?

cert/map lists 4 CREST certifications: 1 beginner, 1 intermediate, 1 advanced, 1 expert.

How much do CREST certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $369 (CPSA) to $805 (CRTIA). 1 of the 4 have no published standalone price or are free.

Which CREST certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and CPSA (CREST Practitioner Security Analyst) is the usual entry point there.

Do CREST certifications expire?

1 of the 4 have a renewal cycle (for example CRTIA, every 3 years); the rest do not expire.

Are CREST exams hands-on?

2 of the 4 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
