MCSI certifications at a glance

How many MCSI certifications are there?

cert/map lists 11 MCSI certifications: 3 beginner, 4 intermediate, 4 advanced.

How much do MCSI certifications cost?

The published exam price is $699 (MOIS). 5 of the 11 have no published standalone price or are free.

Which MCSI certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and MCRTA (MCSI Certified Red Team Analyst) is the usual entry point there.

Do MCSI certifications expire?

None of the MCSI certifications listed here expires.

Are MCSI exams hands-on?

9 of the 11 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
