Where they differ

  • Both exams cost $575.
  • Both sit at expert level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • CISA is recognized under DoD 8140. CRISC is not listed.
  • Both are issued by ISACA.

What each one covers

CISA. Information systems audit certification; the standard for IT auditors.

CRISC. Enterprise risk management certification for risk and control professionals.
