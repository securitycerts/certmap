NYU Introduction to Cyber Security Specialization NYU Intro Cyber

Four-course New York University specialization covering cyber attacks and risk analysis, authentication and cryptography, network security and firewalls, and enterprise security and compliance frameworks. Course-based certificate assessed through quizzes and projects, with no proctored exam.

NYU Intro Cyber is a beginner-level Security & Risk Management certification from Coursera. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It does not expire.
