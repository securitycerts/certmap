Certified Computer Examiner CCE

Digital forensics certification offered by ISFCE since 2003. Applicants need approved training and 18 months of verifiable professional experience for the full designation.

CCE is an intermediate-level Forensics certification from ISFCE. The exam costs $495. It renews every 2 years.
