GIAC Cyber Threat Intelligence GCTI

Cyber threat intelligence analysis certification.

GCTI is an advanced-level Cyber Threat Intelligence certification from GIAC. The exam costs $999. The exam runs for 180 minutes. It renews every 4 years.
