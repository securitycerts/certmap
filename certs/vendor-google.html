Google certifications at a glance

How many Google certifications are there?

cert/map lists 6 Google certifications: 1 beginner, 5 advanced.

How much do Google certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $125 (ACE) to $200 (PSOE).

Which Google certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and ACE (Google Cloud Associate Cloud Engineer) is the usual entry point there.

Do Google certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example ACE every 3 years.

Are Google exams hands-on?

The Google exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
