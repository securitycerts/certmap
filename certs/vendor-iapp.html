IAPP certifications at a glance

How many IAPP certifications are there?

cert/map lists 4 IAPP certifications: 3 intermediate, 1 advanced.

How much do IAPP certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $550 (CIPP) to $799 (AIGP).

Which IAPP certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is intermediate, and CIPP (Certified Information Privacy Professional) is the usual entry point there.

Do IAPP certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example AIGP every 2 years.

Are IAPP exams hands-on?

The IAPP exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
