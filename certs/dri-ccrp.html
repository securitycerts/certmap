Certified Cyber Resilience Professional CCRP

Credential combining cybersecurity and business continuity for resilience professionals. Requires more than two years of experience, essays on three of five elements (identify, protect, detect, respond, recover) and a pass of at least 75% on the Cyber Resilience Examination.

CCRP is an intermediate-level Security & Risk Management certification from DRI International. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It renews every 1 year.
