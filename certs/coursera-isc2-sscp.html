ISC2 Systems Security Certified Practitioner (SSCP) Professional Certificate ISC2 SSCP Prep

Seven-course ISC2 training program covering the SSCP domains, including access controls, security operations, risk identification, incident response, cryptography, and network and systems security. Course-based certificate assessed through knowledge checks and final assessments; the proctored SSCP exam is separate.

ISC2 SSCP Prep is a beginner-level Security Operations certification from Coursera. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It does not expire.
