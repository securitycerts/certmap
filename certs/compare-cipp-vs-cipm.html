The difference between CIPP and CIPM

  • Both exams cost $550.
  • CIPP sits at intermediate level and CIPM at advanced level, so CIPP is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • Both renew every 2 years.
  • Both are issued by IAPP.

What each one covers

CIPP. Privacy law and regulation certification.

CIPM. Privacy program management certification.
