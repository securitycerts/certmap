Practical IoT Pentest Associate PIPA

Associate-level practical review of an embedded Linux IoT device's firmware and hardware captures, with a written report. Formerly the Practical Junior IoT Tester (PJIT); does not expire.

PIPA is an intermediate-level ICS/IoT certification from TCM Security. The exam costs $249. The exam runs for 2 days. It does not expire.
