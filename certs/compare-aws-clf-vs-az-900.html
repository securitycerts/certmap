Where they differ

  • AZ-900 is the cheaper exam: $99 against $100 for CLF-C02, a difference of $1.
  • Both sit at beginner level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CLF-C02 exam is longer: 90 minutes against 45 minutes for AZ-900.
  • CLF-C02: renews every 3 years. AZ-900: does not expire.
