The SecOps Group certifications at a glance

How many The SecOps Group certifications are there?

cert/map lists 23 The SecOps Group certifications: 5 beginner, 12 intermediate, 6 expert.

How much do The SecOps Group certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $134 (CAP) to $537 (CRTeamerX).

Which The SecOps Group certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and CAP (Certified AppSec Practitioner) is the usual entry point there.

Do The SecOps Group certifications expire?

None of the The SecOps Group certifications listed here expires.

Are The SecOps Group exams hands-on?

18 of the 23 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
