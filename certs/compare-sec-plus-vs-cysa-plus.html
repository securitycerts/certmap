The difference between Security+ and CySA+

  • Both exams cost $439.
  • Security+ sits at beginner level and CySA+ at intermediate level, so Security+ is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CySA+ exam is longer: 165 minutes against 90 minutes for Security+.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • Security+ is a prerequisite of CySA+.
  • Both are issued by CompTIA.

What each one covers

Security+. Foundational vendor-neutral cybersecurity certification covering core concepts and hands-on skills.

CySA+. Intermediate security operations certification covering threat detection, vulnerability management and incident response for SOC analysts.
