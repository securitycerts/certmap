Certified DevSecOps Professional CDP

Practical exam of five challenges to be solved in six hours, followed by 24 hours to submit a report, covering security automation in CI/CD pipelines. Lifetime credential.

CDP is an intermediate-level Software Security certification from Practical DevSecOps. The exam costs $899. The exam runs for 360 minutes. It does not expire.
