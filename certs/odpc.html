Offensive Development Practitioner Certification ODPC

Covers development of custom offensive tooling such as shellcode loaders, reflective payloads and process injection, with AMSI and ETW bypass, API unhooking and EDR evasion. Assessed by a 48-hour practical exam in a student-deployed AWS lab followed by 48 hours to submit a report.

ODPC is an advanced-level Exploitation certification from White Knight Labs. The exam costs $700. The exam runs for 2 days. It does not expire.
