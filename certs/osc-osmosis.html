Open-Source Certified OSC

Vendor-neutral OSINT practitioner certification from the OSMOSIS Association with a proctored exam of 100 multiple-choice and true/false questions. Applicants document 75 hours of related experience in the previous year and sign an ethics statement; renewal needs 45 CPEs in three years.

OSC is an intermediate-level OSINT certification from OSMOSIS. The exam costs $395. The exam runs for 90 minutes. It renews every 3 years.
