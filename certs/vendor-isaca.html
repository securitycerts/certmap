ISACA certifications at a glance

How many ISACA certifications are there?

cert/map lists 9 ISACA certifications: 1 intermediate, 4 advanced, 4 expert.

How much do ISACA certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $399 (CCOA) to $575 (CRISC).

Which ISACA certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is intermediate, and CCOA (Certified Cybersecurity Operations Analyst) is the usual entry point there.

Do ISACA certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example CCOA every 3 years.

Are ISACA exams hands-on?

1 of the 9 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
