The difference between A+ and Tech+

  • Tech+ is the cheaper exam: $129 against $274 for A+, a difference of $145.
  • Both sit at beginner level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The A+ exam is longer: 90 minutes against 60 minutes for Tech+.
  • A+: renews every 3 years. Tech+: does not expire.
  • Both are issued by CompTIA.

What each one covers

A+. Entry-level IT support certification covering hardware and operating systems.

Tech+. Entry-level IT fundamentals certification (CompTIA Tech+, FC0-U71); successor to the retired IT Fundamentals+ (ITF+).
