Covers the cloud-native toolchain, DevSecOps practice and security controls in CI/CD pipelines. Exam has 75 questions in two hours.

GCSA is an advanced-level Cloud/SysOps certification from GIAC. The exam costs $999. The exam runs for 120 minutes. It renews every 4 years.
