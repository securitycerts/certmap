Certified iOS Security Researcher CISR

24-hour practical exam on iOS internals, including sandboxing, Mach and XPC IPC, memory management and code signing, assessed through a report with working proofs of concept.

CISR is an advanced-level Exploitation certification from 8kSec. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. The exam runs for 24 hours. It does not expire.
