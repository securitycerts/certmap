Fortinet Industry Certification: OT Security Fortinet OT

Industry certification earned by passing the proctored OT Security Architect exam on top of prerequisite NSE certifications. It replaces the FCSS OT Security title retired on 15 July 2026.

Fortinet OT is an advanced-level ICS/IoT certification from Fortinet. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It renews every 2 years.
