The difference between CCISO and CISM

  • CISM is the cheaper exam: $575 against $999 for CCISO, a difference of $424.
  • Both sit at expert level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CISM exam is longer: 240 minutes against 150 minutes for CCISO.
  • Both renew every 3 years.

What each one covers

CCISO. Executive-level CISO certification covering five governance domains.

CISM. Information security management certification focused on governance and program management.
