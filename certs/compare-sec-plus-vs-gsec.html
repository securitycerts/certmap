The difference between Security+ and GSEC

  • Security+ is the cheaper exam: $439 against $999 for GSEC, a difference of $560.
  • Security+ sits at beginner level and GSEC at intermediate level, so Security+ is the usual place to start.
  • GSEC is assessed with a practical exam. Security+ is a written or multiple-choice exam.
  • The GSEC exam is longer: 240 minutes against 90 minutes for Security+.
  • Security+: renews every 3 years. GSEC: renews every 4 years.

What each one covers

Security+. Foundational vendor-neutral cybersecurity certification covering core concepts and hands-on skills.

GSEC. Hands-on foundational security certification from GIAC.
