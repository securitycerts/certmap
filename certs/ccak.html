Certificate of Cloud Auditing Knowledge CCAK

Cloud auditing certificate jointly issued by CSA and ISACA. Discontinued for new purchases as of October 2025 when the CSA/ISACA partnership ended; existing holders keep their credential.

CCAK is an advanced-level GRC certification from CSA. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. The exam runs for 120 minutes. It renews every 2 years.
