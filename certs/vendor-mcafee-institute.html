McAfee Institute certifications at a glance

How many McAfee Institute certifications are there?

cert/map lists 6 McAfee Institute certifications: 6 intermediate.

How much do McAfee Institute certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $1,397 (CCII) to $2,497 (C|OSINT). 1 of the 6 have no published standalone price or are free.

Which McAfee Institute certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is intermediate, and CCII (Certified Cyber Intelligence Investigator) is the usual entry point there.

Do McAfee Institute certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example C|OSINT every 2 years.

Are McAfee Institute exams hands-on?

The McAfee Institute exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
