The difference between OSCP+ and OSWE

  • Both exams cost $1,749.
  • OSCP+ sits at advanced level and OSWE at expert level, so OSCP+ is the usual place to start.
  • OSCP+ is assessed with a practical exam. OSWE is a written or multiple-choice exam.
  • The OSWE exam is longer: 2 days against 24 hours for OSCP+.
  • OSCP+: renews every 3 years. OSWE: does not expire.
  • OSCP+ is recognized under DoD 8140. OSWE is not listed.
  • Both are issued by OffSec.

What each one covers

OSCP+. Hands-on 24-hour penetration testing exam; industry-respected red team credential.

OSWE. Advanced web application exploitation and white-box code review certification.
