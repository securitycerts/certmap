CSA certifications at a glance

How many CSA certifications are there?

cert/map lists 4 CSA certifications: 3 intermediate, 1 advanced.

How much do CSA certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $175 (CCZT) to $795 (TAISE). 1 of the 4 have no published standalone price or are free.

Which CSA certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is intermediate, and CCSK (Certificate of Cloud Security Knowledge) is the usual entry point there.

Do CSA certifications expire?

1 of the 4 have a renewal cycle (for example CCAK, every 2 years); the rest do not expire.

Are CSA exams hands-on?

The CSA exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
