Master Business Continuity Professional MBCP

Highest DRI business continuity credential, requiring at least five years of experience across seven subject areas, seven essays with references and a pass of at least 75% on both the Qualifying Examination and the Master's Case Study Examination.

MBCP is an expert-level Security & Risk Management certification from DRI International. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It renews every 1 year.
