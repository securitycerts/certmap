Advanced in AI Security Management AAISM

Credential on managing security for AI systems across AI governance, AI risk management and AI technologies and controls. Candidates must hold an active CISM or CISSP; the exam has 90 questions.

AAISM is an advanced-level Security & Risk Management certification from ISACA. The exam costs $459. It renews every 3 years.
