The difference between A+ and CCNA

  • A+ is the cheaper exam: $274 against $300 for CCNA, a difference of $26.
  • A+ sits at beginner level and CCNA at intermediate level, so A+ is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CCNA exam is longer: 120 minutes against 90 minutes for A+.
  • Both renew every 3 years.

What each one covers

A+. Entry-level IT support certification covering hardware and operating systems.

CCNA. Foundational Cisco networking certification.
