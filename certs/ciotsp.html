Certified Internet of Things Security Practitioner CIoTSP

120-minute, 100-item multiple-choice and multiple-response exam (ITS-110) on securing IoT devices, networks, data, software and firmware. Valid for three years.

CIoTSP is an intermediate-level ICS/IoT certification from CertNexus. The exam costs $368. The exam runs for 120 minutes. It renews every 3 years.
