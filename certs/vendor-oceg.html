How many OCEG certifications are there?

How much do OCEG certifications cost?

OCEG does not publish standalone exam prices for the certifications listed here.

Which OCEG certification should I start with?

GRCA is the only OCEG certification on the roadmap. It sits at intermediate level.

Do OCEG certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example GRCA every 1 year.

Are OCEG exams hands-on?

The OCEG exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
