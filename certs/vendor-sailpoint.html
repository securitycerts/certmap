SailPoint certifications at a glance

How many SailPoint certifications are there?

cert/map lists 4 SailPoint certifications: 2 beginner, 2 intermediate.

How much do SailPoint certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $300 (SP-IIQ-A) to $400 (SP-ISE).

Which SailPoint certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and SP-IIQ-A (SailPoint Certified IdentityIQ Associate) is the usual entry point there.

Do SailPoint certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example SP-IIQ-A every 2 years.

Are SailPoint exams hands-on?

The SailPoint exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
