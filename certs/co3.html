Certified Offensive OSINT Operator CO3

Offensive OSINT and reconnaissance certification for red teamers covering attack surface mapping, people and company profiling, OPSEC and automated pipelines. Earned by submitting at least 15 flags in a 30-day practical challenge lab.

CO3 is a beginner-level OSINT certification from CyberWarFare Labs. The exam costs $29. It does not expire.
