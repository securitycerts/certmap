The difference between RHCSA and RHCE

  • Both exams cost $500.
  • RHCSA sits at intermediate level and RHCE at advanced level, so RHCSA is the usual place to start.
  • Both are practical, performance-based exams.
  • The RHCE exam is longer: 240 minutes against 180 minutes for RHCSA.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • RHCSA is a prerequisite of RHCE.
  • Both are issued by Red Hat.

What each one covers

RHCSA. Hands-on Red Hat Enterprise Linux administration certification.

RHCE. Advanced RHEL automation with Ansible certification.
