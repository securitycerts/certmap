Nozomi Networks Certified Engineer NNCE

Certification on deploying and using Nozomi Networks Guardian for OT and IoT network monitoring. Timed online exam of 50 randomized questions in three hours, answered with the help of a dedicated lab environment.

NNCE is an intermediate-level ICS/IoT certification from Nozomi Networks. The exam costs $315. The exam runs for 180 minutes. It does not expire.
