PECB certifications at a glance

How many PECB certifications are there?

cert/map lists 6 PECB certifications: 1 beginner, 2 intermediate, 3 advanced.

How much do PECB certifications cost?

PECB does not publish standalone exam prices for the certifications listed here.

Which PECB certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and ISO 27001 F (PECB Certificate Holder in ISO/IEC 27001 Foundation) is the usual entry point there.

Do PECB certifications expire?

5 of the 6 have a renewal cycle (for example ISO 27001 LA, every 3 years); the rest do not expire.

Are PECB exams hands-on?

The PECB exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
