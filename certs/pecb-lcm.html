PECB Certified Lead Cybersecurity Manager PECB LCM

Certification on implementing and managing a cybersecurity programme. Requires passing the PECB Certified Lead Cybersecurity Manager exam plus two years of cybersecurity experience and 300 hours of cybersecurity activities.

PECB LCM is an advanced-level Security & Risk Management certification from PECB. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It renews every 3 years.
