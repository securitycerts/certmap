Certification for professionals who plan and perform audits of information security management systems against ISO/IEC 27001. Valid for three years with maintenance fees.

ISO 27001 LA is an intermediate-level GRC certification from PECB. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It renews every 3 years.
