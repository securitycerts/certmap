The difference between GICSP and GRID

  • Both exams cost $999.
  • GICSP sits at advanced level and GRID at expert level, so GICSP is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The GICSP exam is longer: 180 minutes against 120 minutes for GRID.
  • Both renew every 4 years.
  • Both are issued by GIAC.

What each one covers

GICSP. Industrial control systems security certification.

GRID. ICS active defense and incident response certification.
