CREST Certified Tester - Application CCT APP

Advanced certification with a three-hour written exam (multiple choice and written scenario) and a three-hour practical exam on web application testing. Accepted by the UK NCSC CHECK scheme.

CCT APP is an expert-level Penetration Testing certification from CREST. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. The exam runs for 360 minutes. It does not expire.
