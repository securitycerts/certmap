Certified in Open Source Intelligence C|OSINT

Board certification in open-source intelligence for investigators, earned by passing the course quizzes and a proctored final exam. Eligibility requires a degree or relevant paid professional experience.

C|OSINT is an intermediate-level OSINT certification from McAfee Institute. The exam costs $2,497. It renews every 2 years.
