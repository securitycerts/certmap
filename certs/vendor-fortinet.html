Fortinet certifications at a glance

How many Fortinet certifications are there?

cert/map lists 6 Fortinet certifications: 1 intermediate, 4 advanced, 1 expert.

How much do Fortinet certifications cost?

The published exam price is $200 (NSE 4). 4 of the 6 have no published standalone price or are free.

Which Fortinet certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is intermediate, and NSE 4 (Fortinet NSE 4 FortiOS Administrator) is the usual entry point there.

Do Fortinet certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example NSE 4 every 2 years.

Are Fortinet exams hands-on?

1 of the 6 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
