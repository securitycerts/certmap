Where they differ

  • Both exams cost $300.
  • Both sit at intermediate level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • CCNA Cybersecurity is recognized under DoD 8140. CCNA is not listed.
  • Both are issued by Cisco.

What each one covers

CCNA. Foundational Cisco networking certification.

CCNA Cybersecurity. Cisco SOC analyst associate certification.
