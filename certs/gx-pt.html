GIAC Experienced Penetration Tester GX-PT

Applied Knowledge exam of 25 CyberLive performance-based challenges in four hours covering network mapping, vulnerability identification and exploitation. Counts toward the GSE portfolio.

GX-PT is an expert-level Penetration Testing certification from GIAC. The exam costs $1,299. The exam runs for 240 minutes. It renews every 4 years.
