The difference between CRTE and CRTO

  • CRTE is the cheaper exam: $299 against $524 for CRTO, a difference of $225.
  • CRTO sits at advanced level and CRTE at expert level, so CRTO is the usual place to start.
  • CRTE is assessed with a practical exam. CRTO is a written or multiple-choice exam.
  • CRTE: renews every 3 years. CRTO: does not expire.

What each one covers

CRTE. Hands-on Active Directory red team certification.

CRTO. Hands-on red team operations certification covering Cobalt Strike tradecraft.
