GIAC Security Expert GSE

Portfolio credential awarded for holding six active GIAC Practitioner certifications and four GIAC Applied Knowledge certifications. It replaced the former multi-day GSE lab exam.

GSE is an expert-level Security Architecture & Engineering certification from GIAC. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It renews every 4 years.
