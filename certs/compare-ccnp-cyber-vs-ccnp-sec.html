The difference between CCNP Cybersecurity and CCNP Security

  • Both exams cost $400.
  • Both sit at advanced level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • CCNP Security is recognized under DoD 8140. CCNP Cybersecurity is not listed.
  • Both are issued by Cisco.

What each one covers

CCNP Cybersecurity. Professional-level security operations certification requiring the 350-201 CBRCOR core exam and one concentration exam (300-215 CBRFIR forensics and incident response, or 300-220 CBRTHD threat hunting).

CCNP Security. Advanced Cisco security professional certification.
