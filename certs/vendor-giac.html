GIAC certifications at a glance

How many GIAC certifications are there?

cert/map lists 53 GIAC certifications: 1 beginner, 13 intermediate, 28 advanced, 11 expert.

How much do GIAC certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $399 (GFACT) to $1,299 (GX-PT). 2 of the 53 have no published standalone price or are free.

Which GIAC certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and GFACT (GIAC Foundational Cybersecurity Technologies) is the usual entry point there.

Do GIAC certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example GFACT every 4 years.

Are GIAC exams hands-on?

16 of the 53 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
