Certified Third Party Risk Assessor CTPRA

Certification for assessors of third-party IT risk controls, requiring five years of related experience and attendance at a live bootcamp. The remotely proctored exam has 120 multiple-choice scenario questions and a 70% pass mark.

CTPRA is an advanced-level GRC certification from Shared Assessments. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It renews every 3 years.
