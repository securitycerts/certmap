EC-Council Certified Threat Intelligence Analyst CTIA

Threat intelligence analyst certification covering CTI lifecycle, collection and analysis.

CTIA is an intermediate-level Cyber Threat Intelligence certification from EC-Council. The exam costs $450. The exam runs for 240 minutes. It renews every 3 years.
