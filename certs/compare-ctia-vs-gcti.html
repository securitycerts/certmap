The difference between CTIA and GCTI

  • CTIA is the cheaper exam: $450 against $999 for GCTI, a difference of $549.
  • CTIA sits at intermediate level and GCTI at advanced level, so CTIA is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CTIA exam is longer: 240 minutes against 180 minutes for GCTI.
  • CTIA: renews every 3 years. GCTI: renews every 4 years.
  • GCTI is recognized under DoD 8140. CTIA is not listed.

What each one covers

CTIA. Threat intelligence analyst certification covering CTI lifecycle, collection and analysis.

GCTI. Cyber threat intelligence analysis certification.
