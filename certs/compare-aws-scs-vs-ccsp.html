The difference between SCS-C03 and CCSP

  • SCS-C03 is the cheaper exam: $300 against $599 for CCSP, a difference of $299.
  • SCS-C03 sits at advanced level and CCSP at expert level, so SCS-C03 is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CCSP exam is longer: 180 minutes against 170 minutes for SCS-C03.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • CCSP is recognized under DoD 8140. SCS-C03 is not listed.

What each one covers

SCS-C03. AWS security specialty certification (exam SCS-C03) covering threat detection, IAM, data protection and incident response.

CCSP. Cloud security architecture and operations certification; requires 5 years experience.
