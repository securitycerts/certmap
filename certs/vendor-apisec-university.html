How many APIsec University certifications are there?

Which APIsec University certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and CASA (Certified API Security Analyst) is the usual entry point there.

Do APIsec University certifications expire?

None of the APIsec University certifications listed here expires.

Are APIsec University exams hands-on?

1 of the 2 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
