The difference between CEH and OSCP+

  • CEH is the cheaper exam: $1,199 against $1,749 for OSCP+, a difference of $550.
  • CEH sits at intermediate level and OSCP+ at advanced level, so CEH is the usual place to start.
  • OSCP+ is assessed with a practical exam. CEH is a written or multiple-choice exam.
  • The OSCP+ exam is longer: 24 hours against 240 minutes for CEH.
  • Both renew every 3 years.

What each one covers

CEH. Broad ethical hacking certification covering attack methodology.

OSCP+. Hands-on 24-hour penetration testing exam; industry-respected red team credential.
