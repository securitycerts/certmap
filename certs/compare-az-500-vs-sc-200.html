The difference between AZ-500 and SC-200

  • SC-200 costs $165. No standalone price is published for AZ-500.
  • Both sit at intermediate level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The AZ-500 exam is longer: 150 minutes against 100 minutes for SC-200.
  • Both renew every year.
  • Both are issued by Microsoft.

What each one covers

AZ-500. Retired on August 31, 2026 and succeeded by the Cloud and AI Security Engineer Associate (SC-500). Covered security controls, identity, network and data protection in Azure.

SC-200. Microsoft Sentinel and Defender SOC analyst certification.
