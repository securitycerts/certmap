Certified Kubernetes Security Specialist CKS

Hands-on Kubernetes security certification covering runtime, supply chain and cluster hardening.

CKS is an advanced-level Cloud/SysOps certification from CNCF. The exam costs $445. The exam runs for 120 minutes. It renews every 2 years.
