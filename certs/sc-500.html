Microsoft Cloud and AI Security Engineer SC-500

Associate certification covering identity, access and governance, security for storage, databases, networking and compute, AI workloads and security posture management in Azure and hybrid environments. Replaced AZ-500 in 2026.

SC-500 is an intermediate-level Cloud/SysOps certification from Microsoft. The exam costs $165. The exam runs for 120 minutes. It renews every 1 year.
