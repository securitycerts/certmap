Where they differ

  • CDSA is the cheaper exam: $210 against $536 for BTL1, a difference of $326.
  • BTL1 sits at beginner level and CDSA at intermediate level, so BTL1 is the usual place to start.
  • Both are practical, performance-based exams.
  • The CDSA exam is longer: 7 days against 24 hours for BTL1.
  • Neither one expires.

What each one covers

BTL1. Junior defensive security certification assessed through a 24-hour practical incident response exam. Issued by Centri, the provider formerly named Security Blue Team.

CDSA. Hands-on defensive SOC analyst certification.
