EC-Council Information Security Analyst Professional Certificate EC-C ISA

Five-course entry-level program covering information security fundamentals, risk assessment, security operations, incident response and threat hunting. Course-based certificate assessed through hands-on labs and projects, with no proctored exam.

EC-C ISA is a beginner-level Security Operations certification from Coursera. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It does not expire.
