Practical Malware Research Professional PMRP

Practical exam on malware analysis and triage that requires an analysis report and detection rules for the samples. Formerly the Practical Junior Malware Researcher (PJMR); does not expire.

PMRP is an intermediate-level Forensics certification from TCM Security. The exam costs $499. The exam runs for 5 days. It does not expire.
