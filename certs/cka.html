Certified Kubernetes Administrator CKA

Hands-on Kubernetes operator certification with a live shell exam.

CKA is an intermediate-level Cloud/SysOps certification from CNCF. The exam costs $445. The exam runs for 120 minutes. It renews every 2 years.
