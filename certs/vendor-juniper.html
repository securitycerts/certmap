Juniper certifications at a glance

How many Juniper certifications are there?

cert/map lists 2 Juniper certifications: 1 beginner, 1 intermediate.

How much do Juniper certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $200 (JNCIA-Junos) to $300 (JNCIS-SEC).

Which Juniper certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and JNCIA-Junos (Juniper Networks Certified Associate Junos) is the usual entry point there.

Do Juniper certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example JNCIA-Junos every 3 years.

Are Juniper exams hands-on?

The Juniper exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
