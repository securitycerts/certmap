The difference between CKA and CKS

  • Both exams cost $445.
  • CKA sits at intermediate level and CKS at advanced level, so CKA is the usual place to start.
  • Both are practical, performance-based exams.
  • Both renew every 2 years.
  • CKA is a prerequisite of CKS.
  • Both are issued by CNCF.

What each one covers

CKA. Hands-on Kubernetes operator certification with a live shell exam.

CKS. Hands-on Kubernetes security certification covering runtime, supply chain and cluster hardening.
