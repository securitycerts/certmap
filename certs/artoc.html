Advanced Red Team Operations Certification ARTOC

Covers C2 operations with Cobalt Strike and Havoc, redirector and cloud infrastructure, Active Directory and AD CS exploitation, lateral movement, persistence and EDR evasion. Assessed by a 48-hour practical exam requiring C2 deployment, target compromise and flags, followed by 48 hours to submit a report.

ARTOC is an advanced-level Penetration Testing certification from White Knight Labs. The exam costs $700. The exam runs for 2 days. It does not expire.
