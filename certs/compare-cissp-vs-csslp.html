The difference between CISSP and CSSLP

  • CSSLP is the cheaper exam: $599 against $749 for CISSP, a difference of $150.
  • Both sit at expert level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • Both are issued by ISC2.

What each one covers

CISSP. Gold-standard cybersecurity certification covering 8 domains; requires 5 years experience.

CSSLP. Secure software development lifecycle certification for developers and architects.
