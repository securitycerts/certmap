CrowdStrike certifications at a glance

How many CrowdStrike certifications are there?

cert/map lists 5 CrowdStrike certifications: 4 intermediate, 1 advanced.

How much do CrowdStrike certifications cost?

CrowdStrike does not publish standalone exam prices for the certifications listed here.

Which CrowdStrike certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is intermediate, and CCCS (CrowdStrike Certified Cloud Specialist) is the usual entry point there.

Do CrowdStrike certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example CCCS every 3 years.

Are CrowdStrike exams hands-on?

The CrowdStrike exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
