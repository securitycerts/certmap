Cisco Cybersecurity Operations Fundamentals Specialization Cisco CyberOps

Seven-course Cisco specialization covering security operations center basics, network and endpoint monitoring, attack analysis and incident handling for entry-level analysts. Course-based certificate assessed through quizzes, with no projects and no proctored exam.

Cisco CyberOps is a beginner-level Security Operations certification from Coursera. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It does not expire.
