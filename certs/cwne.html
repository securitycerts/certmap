Certified Wireless Network Expert CWNE

Top CWNP credential awarded by board review, not by exam. Applicants must hold the CWNP professional certifications plus CWISA and one external networking certification, and submit essays, publications and three endorsements; renewal requires 60 continuing education credits.

CWNE is an expert-level Communication & Network Security certification from CWNP. The exam costs $500. It does not expire.
