ISACA Certified Data Privacy Solutions Engineer CDPSE

ISACA privacy engineering certification covering governance, architecture and lifecycle of personal data.

CDPSE is an advanced-level GRC certification from ISACA. The exam costs $575. The exam runs for 210 minutes. It renews every 3 years.
