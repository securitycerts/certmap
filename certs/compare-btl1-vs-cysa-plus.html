The difference between BTL1 and CySA+

  • CySA+ is the cheaper exam: $439 against $536 for BTL1, a difference of $97.
  • BTL1 sits at beginner level and CySA+ at intermediate level, so BTL1 is the usual place to start.
  • BTL1 is assessed with a practical exam. CySA+ is a written or multiple-choice exam.
  • The BTL1 exam is longer: 24 hours against 165 minutes for CySA+.
  • BTL1: does not expire. CySA+: renews every 3 years.
  • CySA+ is recognized under DoD 8140. BTL1 is not listed.

What each one covers

BTL1. Junior defensive security certification assessed through a 24-hour practical incident response exam. Issued by Centri, the provider formerly named Security Blue Team.

CySA+. Intermediate security operations certification covering threat detection, vulnerability management and incident response for SOC analysts.
