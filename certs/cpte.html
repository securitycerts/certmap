Certified Penetration Testing Engineer C)PTE

Penetration testing certification assessed by a 100-question online exam with no course prerequisite. Sold as an unproctored standard exam or as a live-proctored ANAB-accredited version (C)PTE-A, two hours, 62% to pass, renewed every three years).

C)PTE is an intermediate-level Penetration Testing certification from Mile2. The exam costs $358. It does not expire.
