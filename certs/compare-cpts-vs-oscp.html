The difference between CPTS and OSCP+

  • CPTS is the cheaper exam: $210 against $1,749 for OSCP+, a difference of $1,539.
  • Both sit at advanced level on the roadmap.
  • Both are practical, performance-based exams.
  • The CPTS exam is longer: 10 days against 24 hours for OSCP+.
  • CPTS: does not expire. OSCP+: renews every 3 years.
  • OSCP+ is recognized under DoD 8140. CPTS is not listed.

What each one covers

CPTS. Hands-on penetration testing certification with 7-day exam and report.

OSCP+. Hands-on 24-hour penetration testing exam; industry-respected red team credential.
