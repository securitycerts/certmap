The difference between CCISO and CISSP

  • CISSP is the cheaper exam: $749 against $999 for CCISO, a difference of $250.
  • Both sit at expert level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CISSP exam is longer: 180 minutes against 150 minutes for CCISO.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • CISSP is recognized under DoD 8140. CCISO is not listed.

What each one covers

CCISO. Executive-level CISO certification covering five governance domains.

CISSP. Gold-standard cybersecurity certification covering 8 domains; requires 5 years experience.
