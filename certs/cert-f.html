Certified Evidence Repair Technician - Forensic CERT-F

Mobile device repair for forensic extraction, covering disassembly, micro-soldering and replacement of ports, screens and buttons. The course page states the credential is earned by passing a practical competency exam at the end of the 5-day in-person course.

CERT-F is an expert-level Forensics certification from Cellebrite. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It does not expire.
