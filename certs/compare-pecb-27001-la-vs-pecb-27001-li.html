The difference between ISO 27001 LA and ISO 27001 LI

  • Neither vendor publishes a standalone exam price.
  • Both sit at intermediate level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • Both are issued by PECB.

What each one covers

ISO 27001 LA. Certification for professionals who plan and perform audits of information security management systems against ISO/IEC 27001. Valid for three years with maintenance fees.

ISO 27001 LI. Certification for professionals who establish, implement and maintain an information security management system based on ISO/IEC 27001. Valid for three years with maintenance fees.
