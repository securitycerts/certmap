Where they differ

  • CCSK is the cheaper exam: $445 against $599 for CCSP, a difference of $154.
  • CCSK sits at intermediate level and CCSP at expert level, so CCSK is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CCSP exam is longer: 180 minutes against 120 minutes for CCSK.
  • CCSK: does not expire. CCSP: renews every 3 years.
  • CCSP is recognized under DoD 8140. CCSK is not listed.

What each one covers

CCSK. Vendor-neutral foundational cloud security certificate from CSA.

CCSP. Cloud security architecture and operations certification; requires 5 years experience.
