The difference between CWEE and OSWE

  • CWEE is the cheaper exam: $350 against $1,749 for OSWE, a difference of $1,399.
  • Both sit at expert level on the roadmap.
  • CWEE is assessed with a practical exam. OSWE is a written or multiple-choice exam.
  • The CWEE exam is longer: 10 days against 2 days for OSWE.
  • Neither one expires.

What each one covers

CWEE. Hands-on advanced web exploitation certification.

OSWE. Advanced web application exploitation and white-box code review certification.
