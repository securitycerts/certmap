Certified Business Continuity Professional CBCP

Business continuity credential requiring more than two years of experience, five subject-area essays with references and a pass of at least 75% on the DRI Qualifying Examination.

CBCP is an intermediate-level Security & Risk Management certification from DRI International. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. The exam runs for 150 minutes. It renews every 1 year.
