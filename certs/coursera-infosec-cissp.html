Infosec Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) Specialization Infosec CISSP

Eight-course Infosec specialization reviewing the CISSP domains, with end-of-course assessments and a final practice exam. Course-based exam-preparation certificate; the proctored ISC2 CISSP exam is separate.

Infosec CISSP is an advanced-level Security & Risk Management certification from Coursera. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It does not expire.
