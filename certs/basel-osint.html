Basel LEARN Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) Basel OSINT

Free self-paced OSINT course for financial and corruption investigators built around a simulated illegal fishing case that ends with an OSINT report. It awards a certificate of completion from the International Centre for Asset Recovery rather than an examined certification, and is widely used in the asset recovery and OSINT community.

Basel OSINT is a beginner-level OSINT certification from Basel Institute. It is free. It does not expire.
