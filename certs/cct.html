Certified Cybersecurity Technician CCT

Entry-level cybersecurity technician certification (exam 212-82). The exam has 60 questions, 50 multiple choice and 10 practical, in 3 hours.

CCT is a beginner-level Security Operations certification from EC-Council. The exam costs $499. The exam runs for 180 minutes. It renews every 3 years.
