OSINT Industries OSINT Training OSINT Ind.

Practitioner-led OSINT training of about 18 hours over three days, built from modules such as OSINT Fundamentals, Advanced OSINT and OSINT for ICAC analysts. Course-based, with hands-on exercises; some courses end in an assessment such as a CTF or a quiz.

OSINT Ind. is an intermediate-level OSINT certification from OSINT Industries. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It does not expire.
