Blue Team Level 2 BTL2

Advanced defensive certification with a practical assessment of up to 72 hours and a written report that is marked by hand. Issued by Centri, the provider formerly named Security Blue Team.

BTL2 is an advanced-level Incident Handling certification from Centri. The exam costs $2,683. The exam runs for 3 days. It does not expire.
