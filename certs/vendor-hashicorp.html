HashiCorp certifications at a glance

How many HashiCorp certifications are there?

cert/map lists 2 HashiCorp certifications: 1 beginner, 1 advanced.

How much do HashiCorp certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $71 (Vault Associate) to $295 (Vault Ops Adv).

Which HashiCorp certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and Vault Associate (HashiCorp Certified: Vault Associate) is the usual entry point there.

Do HashiCorp certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example Vault Associate every 2 years.

Are HashiCorp exams hands-on?

1 of the 2 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
