PECB Certified ISO 31000 Lead Risk Manager ISO 31000 LRM

Certification on establishing and running a risk management framework and process based on ISO 31000. Requires passing the PECB exam plus five years of professional experience, two of them in risk management, and 300 hours of risk management activities.

ISO 31000 LRM is an advanced-level Security & Risk Management certification from PECB. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It renews every 3 years.
