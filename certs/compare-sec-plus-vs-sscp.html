The difference between Security+ and SSCP

  • SSCP is the cheaper exam: $249 against $439 for Security+, a difference of $190.
  • Security+ sits at beginner level and SSCP at intermediate level, so Security+ is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The SSCP exam is longer: 120 minutes against 90 minutes for Security+.
  • Both renew every 3 years.

What each one covers

Security+. Foundational vendor-neutral cybersecurity certification covering core concepts and hands-on skills.

SSCP. Hands-on security operations certification for IT administrators.
