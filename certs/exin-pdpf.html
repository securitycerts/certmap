EXIN Privacy & Data Protection Foundation PDPF

Foundation exam on the GDPR: scope, principles and practice of data processing, international transfers and risk assessment. Closed-book, 40 multiple-choice questions in one hour with a 65% pass mark.

PDPF is a beginner-level GRC certification from EXIN. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. The exam runs for 60 minutes. It does not expire.
