The difference between OSEP and OSWE

  • Both exams cost $1,749.
  • Both sit at expert level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • Neither one expires.
  • Both are issued by OffSec.

What each one covers

OSEP. Advanced evasion and bypass techniques for mature defensive environments.

OSWE. Advanced web application exploitation and white-box code review certification.
