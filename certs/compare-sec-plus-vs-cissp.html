The difference between Security+ and CISSP

  • Security+ is the cheaper exam: $439 against $749 for CISSP, a difference of $310.
  • Security+ sits at beginner level and CISSP at expert level, so Security+ is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CISSP exam is longer: 180 minutes against 90 minutes for Security+.
  • Both renew every 3 years.

What each one covers

Security+. Foundational vendor-neutral cybersecurity certification covering core concepts and hands-on skills.

CISSP. Gold-standard cybersecurity certification covering 8 domains; requires 5 years experience.
