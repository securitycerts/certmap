90-minute, 60-question exam on threat hunting with CrowdStrike Falcon telemetry and queries. Valid for three years.

CCFH is an advanced-level Security Operations certification from CrowdStrike. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. The exam runs for 90 minutes. It renews every 3 years.
