CWNP certifications at a glance

How many CWNP certifications are there?

cert/map lists 2 CWNP certifications: 1 beginner, 1 intermediate.

How much do CWNP certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $275 (CWNA) to $350 (CWSP).

Which CWNP certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and CWNA (Certified Wireless Network Administrator) is the usual entry point there.

Do CWNP certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example CWNA every 3 years.

Are CWNP exams hands-on?

The CWNP exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
