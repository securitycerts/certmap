ISC2 certifications at a glance

How many ISC2 certifications are there?

cert/map lists 9 ISC2 certifications: 1 beginner, 1 intermediate, 1 advanced, 6 expert.

How much do ISC2 certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $199 (CC) to $749 (CISSP).

Which ISC2 certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and CC (Certified in Cybersecurity) is the usual entry point there.

Do ISC2 certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example CC every 3 years.

Are ISC2 exams hands-on?

The ISC2 exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
