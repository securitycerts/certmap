CertNexus certifications at a glance

How many CertNexus certifications are there?

cert/map lists 3 CertNexus certifications: 1 beginner, 2 intermediate.

How much do CertNexus certifications cost?

The published exam price is $368 (CIoTP).

Which CertNexus certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and CIoTP (Certified Internet of Things Practitioner) is the usual entry point there.

Do CertNexus certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example CIoTP every 3 years.

Are CertNexus exams hands-on?

The CertNexus exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
