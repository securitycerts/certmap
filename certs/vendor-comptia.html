CompTIA certifications at a glance

How many CompTIA certifications are there?

cert/map lists 15 CompTIA certifications: 6 beginner, 7 intermediate, 2 advanced.

How much do CompTIA certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $129 (Tech+) to $544 (CloudNetX).

Which CompTIA certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and Security+ (CompTIA Security+) is the usual entry point there.

Do CompTIA certifications expire?

12 of the 15 have a renewal cycle (for example A+, every 3 years); the rest do not expire.

Are CompTIA exams hands-on?

1 of the 15 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
