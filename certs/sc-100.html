Microsoft Cybersecurity Architect SC-100

Microsoft cybersecurity architect expert certification (exam SC-100); requires one associate prerequisite such as AZ-500, SC-200 or SC-300.

SC-100 is an expert-level Security Architecture & Engineering certification from Microsoft. The exam costs $165. The exam runs for 150 minutes. It renews every 1 year.
