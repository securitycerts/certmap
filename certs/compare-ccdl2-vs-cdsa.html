The difference between CCDL2 and CDSA

  • CDSA is the cheaper exam: $210 against $850 for CCDL2, a difference of $640.
  • Both sit at intermediate level on the roadmap.
  • Both are practical, performance-based exams.
  • The CDSA exam is longer: 7 days against 2 days for CCDL2.
  • CCDL2: does not expire. CDSA: does not expire.

What each one covers

CCDL2. Threat hunting and DFIR certification, formerly named CCD, assessed through a 48-hour practical exam covering disk and memory forensics, network analysis and SIEM hunting.

CDSA. Hands-on defensive SOC analyst certification.
