Certified Red Team Analyst CRTA

Entry-level red team certification with a six-hour practical exam covering external and internal operations against an Active Directory environment.

CRTA is a beginner-level Penetration Testing certification from CyberWarFare Labs. The exam costs $69. The exam runs for 360 minutes. It does not expire.
