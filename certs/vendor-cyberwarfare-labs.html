CyberWarFare Labs certifications at a glance

How many CyberWarFare Labs certifications are there?

cert/map lists 21 CyberWarFare Labs certifications: 7 beginner, 8 intermediate, 6 advanced.

How much do CyberWarFare Labs certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $9 (CRT-COI) to $199 (CHMRTS).

Which CyberWarFare Labs certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and API-RTA (API Red Team Analyst) is the usual entry point there.

Do CyberWarFare Labs certifications expire?

None of the CyberWarFare Labs certifications listed here expires.

Are CyberWarFare Labs exams hands-on?

20 of the 21 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
