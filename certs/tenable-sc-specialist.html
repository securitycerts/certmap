Tenable Security Center Specialist Tenable SC

Specialist certification for the Tenable Security Center product. Requires a proctored written exam (60 multiple-choice questions, 120 minutes) and then a practical exam (up to 30 tasks, 240 minutes), each with an 80% pass mark; recertification repeats both exams.

Tenable SC is an intermediate-level Security Operations certification from Tenable. The exam costs $540. The exam runs for 360 minutes. It renews every 2 years.
