Microsoft Information Security Administrator SC-401

Microsoft information security administrator certification (exam SC-401, replacing the retired SC-400); covers information protection, DLP, retention and insider risk with Microsoft Purview.

SC-401 is an intermediate-level GRC certification from Microsoft. The exam costs $165. The exam runs for 100 minutes. It renews every 1 year.
