CrowdStrike Certified Identity Specialist CCIS

90-minute, 60-question exam on Falcon Identity Protection, covering identity-based detections, policy rules, user risk and Zero Trust principles. Valid for three years.

CCIS is an intermediate-level IAM certification from CrowdStrike. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. The exam runs for 90 minutes. It renews every 3 years.
