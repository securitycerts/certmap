CrowdStrike Certified Falcon Responder CCFR

90-minute, 60-question exam on triaging and responding to detections in the CrowdStrike Falcon platform. Valid for three years.

CCFR is an intermediate-level Incident Handling certification from CrowdStrike. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. The exam runs for 90 minutes. It renews every 3 years.
