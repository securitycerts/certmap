The difference between GCIH and GCFA

  • Both exams cost $999.
  • GCIH sits at intermediate level and GCFA at advanced level, so GCIH is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The GCIH exam is longer: 240 minutes against 180 minutes for GCFA.
  • Both renew every 4 years.
  • Both are issued by GIAC.

What each one covers

GCIH. Incident response and handler certification covering attacker techniques and IR process.

GCFA. Advanced digital forensics and incident response certification.
