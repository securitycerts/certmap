The difference between CISM and CISSP

  • CISM is the cheaper exam: $575 against $749 for CISSP, a difference of $174.
  • Both sit at expert level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CISM exam is longer: 240 minutes against 180 minutes for CISSP.
  • Both renew every 3 years.

What each one covers

CISM. Information security management certification focused on governance and program management.

CISSP. Gold-standard cybersecurity certification covering 8 domains; requires 5 years experience.
