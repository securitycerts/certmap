The difference between SCS-C03 and CCSK

  • SCS-C03 is the cheaper exam: $300 against $445 for CCSK, a difference of $145.
  • CCSK sits at intermediate level and SCS-C03 at advanced level, so CCSK is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The SCS-C03 exam is longer: 170 minutes against 120 minutes for CCSK.
  • SCS-C03: renews every 3 years. CCSK: does not expire.

What each one covers

SCS-C03. AWS security specialty certification (exam SCS-C03) covering threat detection, IAM, data protection and incident response.

CCSK. Vendor-neutral foundational cloud security certificate from CSA.
