The difference between CISA and CIA

  • CISA is the cheaper exam: $575 against $1,515 for CIA, a difference of $940.
  • CIA sits at advanced level and CISA at expert level, so CIA is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CIA exam is longer: 390 minutes against 240 minutes for CISA.
  • CISA: renews every 3 years. CIA: renews every year.
  • CISA is recognized under DoD 8140. CIA is not listed.

What each one covers

CISA. Information systems audit certification; the standard for IT auditors.

CIA. Internal audit certification earned by passing three exam parts (125 questions in 150 minutes, then 100 questions in 120 minutes each) and meeting degree and internal audit experience requirements. Holders attest to continuing education every year.
