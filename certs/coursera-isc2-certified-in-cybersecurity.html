ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity Specialization ISC2 CC Spec

Five-course ISC2 specialization covering security principles, incident response and business continuity, access control, network security and security operations, aligned to the Certified in Cybersecurity exam domains. Course-based certificate assessed through quizzes and case studies; the proctored CC exam is separate.

ISC2 CC Spec is a beginner-level Security & Risk Management certification from Coursera. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. It does not expire.
