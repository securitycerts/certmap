ITIL 4 Practitioner: Information Security Management ITIL4-ISM

Practice-level ITIL 4 certification on managing risks to the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information. Closed-book exam of 20 multiple-choice questions in 30 minutes with a 65% pass mark; accredited training or official eLearning is mandatory.

ITIL4-ISM is an intermediate-level GRC certification from PeopleCert. The exam costs $504. The exam runs for 30 minutes. It renews every 3 years.
