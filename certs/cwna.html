Certified Wireless Network Administrator CWNA

Vendor-neutral enterprise Wi-Fi administration certification with a 60-question exam. It is the required base credential for the CWNP professional-level certifications.

CWNA is a beginner-level Communication & Network Security certification from CWNP. The exam costs $275. The exam runs for 90 minutes. It renews every 3 years.
