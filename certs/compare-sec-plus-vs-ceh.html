The difference between Security+ and CEH

  • Security+ is the cheaper exam: $439 against $1,199 for CEH, a difference of $760.
  • Security+ sits at beginner level and CEH at intermediate level, so Security+ is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CEH exam is longer: 240 minutes against 90 minutes for Security+.
  • Both renew every 3 years.

What each one covers

Security+. Foundational vendor-neutral cybersecurity certification covering core concepts and hands-on skills.

CEH. Broad ethical hacking certification covering attack methodology.
