AWS Cloud Red Team Specialist CARTS

Red team certification for AWS covering attack surface enumeration, exploitation of misconfigured services and pivoting across VPCs. Earned by submitting at least 10 flags in a 30-day practical challenge lab.

CARTS is an intermediate-level Cloud/SysOps certification from CyberWarFare Labs. The exam costs $49. It does not expire.
