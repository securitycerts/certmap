The difference between AZ-900 and AZ-104

  • AZ-900 is the cheaper exam: $99 against $165 for AZ-104, a difference of $66.
  • AZ-900 sits at beginner level and AZ-104 at intermediate level, so AZ-900 is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The AZ-104 exam is longer: 100 minutes against 45 minutes for AZ-900.
  • AZ-900: does not expire. AZ-104: renews every year.
  • Both are issued by Microsoft.
