Elite penetration testing credential awarded for scoring 90 percent or higher on EC-Council's 24-hour online proctored CPENT live-range exam.

LPT is an expert-level Penetration Testing certification from EC-Council. The exam costs $999. The exam runs for 24 hours. It renews every 3 years.
