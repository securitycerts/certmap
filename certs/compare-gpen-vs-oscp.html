The difference between GPEN and OSCP+

  • GPEN is the cheaper exam: $999 against $1,749 for OSCP+, a difference of $750.
  • Both sit at advanced level on the roadmap.
  • OSCP+ is assessed with a practical exam. GPEN is a written or multiple-choice exam.
  • The OSCP+ exam is longer: 24 hours against 180 minutes for GPEN.
  • GPEN: renews every 4 years. OSCP+: renews every 3 years.
  • GPEN is recognized under DoD 8140. OSCP+ is not listed.

What each one covers

GPEN. Penetration testing methodology and exploitation certification.

OSCP+. Hands-on 24-hour penetration testing exam; industry-respected red team credential.
