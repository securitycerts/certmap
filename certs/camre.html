24-hour practical exam on reverse engineering Android malware samples in a Corellium lab, assessed through a written report of findings and countermeasures.

CAMRE is an advanced-level Forensics certification from 8kSec. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. The exam runs for 24 hours. It does not expire.
