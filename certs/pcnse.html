Palo Alto Networks Certified Network Security Engineer PCNSE

Retired in July 2025 and succeeded by Palo Alto Networks role-based credentials (Next-Generation Firewall Engineer, Network Security Professional). Covered PAN-OS configuration, deployment and troubleshooting.

PCNSE is an advanced-level Communication & Network Security certification from Palo Alto Networks. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. The exam runs for 80 minutes. It renews every 2 years.
