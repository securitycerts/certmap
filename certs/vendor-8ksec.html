8kSec certifications at a glance

How many 8kSec certifications are there?

cert/map lists 11 8kSec certifications: 8 advanced, 3 expert.

How much do 8kSec certifications cost?

8kSec does not publish standalone exam prices for the certifications listed here.

Which 8kSec certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is advanced, and CAISR (Certified AI Security Researcher) is the usual entry point there.

Do 8kSec certifications expire?

None of the 8kSec certifications listed here expires.

Are 8kSec exams hands-on?

Yes. Every 8kSec exam listed here is practical and performance-based.
