The difference between CWES and CPTS

  • Both exams cost $210.
  • CWES sits at intermediate level and CPTS at advanced level, so CWES is the usual place to start.
  • Both are practical, performance-based exams.
  • The CPTS exam is longer: 10 days against 7 days for CWES.
  • Neither one expires.
  • Both are issued by Hack The Box.

What each one covers

CWES. HTB Certified Web Exploitation Specialist (CWES), formerly Certified Bug Bounty Hunter. Hands-on web application and API exploitation certification with a practical exam.

CPTS. Hands-on penetration testing certification with 7-day exam and report.
