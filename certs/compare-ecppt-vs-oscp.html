The difference between eCPPT and OSCP+

  • eCPPT is the cheaper exam: $450 against $1,749 for OSCP+, a difference of $1,299.
  • eCPPT sits at intermediate level and OSCP+ at advanced level, so eCPPT is the usual place to start.
  • Both are practical, performance-based exams.
  • Both renew every 3 years.

What each one covers

eCPPT. Practical penetration testing exam for working penetration testers. The credential is valid for three years.

OSCP+. Hands-on 24-hour penetration testing exam; industry-respected red team credential.
