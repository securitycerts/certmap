The difference between Security+ and CCNA

  • CCNA is the cheaper exam: $300 against $439 for Security+, a difference of $139.
  • Security+ sits at beginner level and CCNA at intermediate level, so Security+ is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CCNA exam is longer: 120 minutes against 90 minutes for Security+.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • Security+ is recognized under DoD 8140. CCNA is not listed.

What each one covers

Security+. Foundational vendor-neutral cybersecurity certification covering core concepts and hands-on skills.

CCNA. Foundational Cisco networking certification.
