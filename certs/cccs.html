CrowdStrike Certified Cloud Specialist CCCS

90-minute, 60-question exam on configuring and operating Falcon Cloud Security, covering cloud account registration, policies, runtime protection and detection analysis. Valid for three years.

CCCS is an intermediate-level Cloud/SysOps certification from CrowdStrike. The vendor does not publish a standalone exam price. The exam runs for 90 minutes. It renews every 3 years.
