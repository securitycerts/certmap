Where they differ

  • Google Cyber is the cheaper exam: $294 against $439 for Security+, a difference of $145.
  • Both sit at beginner level on the roadmap.
  • Google Cyber is assessed with a practical exam. Security+ is a written or multiple-choice exam.
  • Google Cyber: does not expire. Security+: renews every 3 years.
  • Security+ is recognized under DoD 8140. Google Cyber is not listed.

What each one covers

Google Cyber. Nine-course entry-level program covering security frameworks, network security, Linux, SQL, SIEM and IDS tools, incident detection and response, and Python automation. Course-based certificate assessed through quizzes, hands-on labs and portfolio activities, with no proctored exam.

Security+. Foundational vendor-neutral cybersecurity certification covering core concepts and hands-on skills.
