GIAC Cloud Penetration Tester GCPN

Covers cloud penetration testing fundamentals, AWS and Azure attacks, and cloud-native applications with containers and CI/CD pipelines. Exam has 75 questions in two hours.

GCPN is an advanced-level Cloud/SysOps certification from GIAC. The exam costs $999. The exam runs for 120 minutes. It renews every 4 years.
