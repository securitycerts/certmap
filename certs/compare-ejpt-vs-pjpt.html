The difference between eJPT and PJPT

  • PJPT is the cheaper exam: $249 against $250 for eJPT, a difference of $1.
  • Both sit at beginner level on the roadmap.
  • PJPT is assessed with a practical exam. eJPT is a written or multiple-choice exam.
  • eJPT: renews every 3 years. PJPT: does not expire.

What each one covers

eJPT. Entry-level practical penetration testing certification.

PJPT. Entry-level practical internal network penetration testing certification.
