Palo Alto Networks certifications at a glance

How many Palo Alto Networks certifications are there?

cert/map lists 9 Palo Alto Networks certifications: 3 intermediate, 5 advanced, 1 expert.

How much do Palo Alto Networks certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $200 (CloudSec Pro) to $300 (NetSec Architect). 2 of the 9 have no published standalone price or are free.

Which Palo Alto Networks certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is intermediate, and NetSec Pro (Palo Alto Networks Network Security Professional) is the usual entry point there.

Do Palo Alto Networks certifications expire?

Yes. All of them have a renewal cycle, for example CloudSec Pro every 2 years.

Are Palo Alto Networks exams hands-on?

The Palo Alto Networks exams listed here are written or multiple choice.
