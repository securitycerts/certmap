The difference between CySA+ and CASP+

  • CySA+ is the cheaper exam: $439 against $544 for CASP+, a difference of $105.
  • CySA+ sits at intermediate level and CASP+ at advanced level, so CySA+ is the usual place to start.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • Both are issued by CompTIA.

What each one covers

CySA+. Intermediate security operations certification covering threat detection, vulnerability management and incident response for SOC analysts.

CASP+. Advanced security practitioner certification covering enterprise security architecture.
