The difference between CC and Security+

  • CC is the cheaper exam: $199 against $439 for Security+, a difference of $240.
  • Both sit at beginner level on the roadmap.
  • Both are written or multiple-choice exams.
  • The CC exam is longer: 120 minutes against 90 minutes for Security+.
  • Both renew every 3 years.
  • Security+ is recognized under DoD 8140. CC is not listed.

What each one covers

CC. Entry-level cybersecurity certification covering foundational security principles; no work experience required.

Security+. Foundational vendor-neutral cybersecurity certification covering core concepts and hands-on skills.
