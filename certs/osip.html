Open Source Intelligence Professional OSIP

Practical certification in which the candidate receives a simulated client request and has 10 days to deliver an investigation case file, graded pass or fail against a professional standard. It does not expire.

OSIP is an advanced-level OSINT certification from IntelTechniques. The exam costs $300. The exam runs for 10 days. It does not expire.
