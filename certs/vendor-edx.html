edX certifications at a glance

How many edX certifications are there?

cert/map lists 13 edX certifications: 7 beginner, 5 intermediate, 1 advanced.

How much do edX certifications cost?

Published exam prices run from $122 (Google SecOps) to $1,697 (IBM Analyst edX).

Which edX certification should I start with?

The lowest tier on the roadmap is beginner, and CS50 Cyber (HarvardX CS50's Introduction to Cybersecurity) is the usual entry point there.

Do edX certifications expire?

None of the edX certifications listed here expires.

Are edX exams hands-on?

4 of the 13 are assessed with a practical, performance-based exam; the others are written or multiple choice.
