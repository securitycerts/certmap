# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be civil, keep discussions on the technical merits, and assume good faith. Personal attacks, harassment and discriminatory language will get you removed from the project's communication channels and have your contributions reverted.

Enforcement actions are taken at the maintainers' discretion. To report behaviour that violates this code, email the maintainer privately. Reports will be handled confidentially.
