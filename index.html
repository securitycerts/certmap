What it is

The roadmap places 320 certifications from 70 vendors on a grid of 17 domains and four seniority levels, from entry-level credentials such as CompTIA Security+ to expert ones such as OSCE3 and CISSP. Each entry lists the current exam price in US dollars, the length and format of the exam, the renewal cycle and the prerequisites, checked against the vendor's own page.

Pick the certifications you are aiming for and the site orders them by prerequisite, adds up the exam cost and the study time, and gives you a link to share the result. The project is open source, the dataset is free to reuse and corrections are welcome.

How to use it

  1. ExploreScan the matrix by domain and level, or filter by vendor, price, exam format and DoD 8140 status.
  2. SelectAdd the certifications you want. Prerequisites can be pulled in with one click.
  3. PlanSee the path in order, with the total exam cost and an estimate of the study time.
  4. StudyFollow each certification's study plan: exam guide, courses, free labs and practice questions, numbered in order.
  5. ShareName the roadmap and copy its link. Anyone who opens it sees the same path.

Independent by design

No ads, no tracking, nothing for sale in the data cert/map is funded by a small number of sponsors: organisations that train, hire or equip security professionals. Sponsorship is by arrangement and never touches what is listed.

Questions and answers

What is a cybersecurity certification roadmap?

A roadmap lays certifications out by subject and by seniority so you can see which ones lead to which. cert/map places 320 certifications from 70 vendors on a grid of 17 domains and four levels, from beginner to expert, and shows the price, exam format and prerequisites of each.

Which cybersecurity certification should a beginner start with?

The most widely recognised entry-level certifications on the roadmap are Security+ ($439), CC ($199), A+ ($274), ACE ($125), AZ-900 ($99). Which one fits depends on the direction you want to take: general security, networking, cloud or hands-on testing.

How much do cybersecurity certifications cost?

Exam prices on the roadmap run from free to $2,683. The cheapest paid exams are CO3 ($29), CRTA ($69), Vault Associate ($71), and the most expensive are BTL2 ($2,683), C|OSINT ($2,497), CSMIE ($1,997). Prices are list prices in US dollars for the exam, without training unless the vendor only sells them together.

Are there free cybersecurity certifications?

Yes. Basel OSINT, CTI 101, OSCE3 have no exam fee.

Which certifications have a hands-on exam?

103 of the 320 certifications are assessed with a practical exam in a lab rather than multiple choice, among them Cisco EH, MCRTA, OSCC-SEC, PJPT, CCO, CDSA.

Which certifications count for DoD 8140?

31 certifications on the roadmap are recognised under the US Department of Defense 8140 framework, including Network+, Security+, CCNA Cybersecurity, CEH, CHFI, CySA+.

How current are the prices?

258 of 320 prices carry the date they were last checked against the vendor's own page; the most recent check was on 2026-11-10. Each certification page shows its own date. Vendors change prices without notice, so confirm before paying.

Does cert/map help me study for a certification?

Yes. 79 certifications have a study plan: the exam guide first, then courses and reading, then hands-on practice with direct links to free labs and open-source projects, then practice questions. The plans hold 653 links in total, and every link was opened when it was added.

Can I build and share my own certification path?

Yes. Select certifications in the interactive roadmap and it orders them by prerequisite and adds up exam cost and study time. Give the path a name and copy its link: anyone who opens it sees the same roadmap.
