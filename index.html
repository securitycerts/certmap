About this cybersecurity certification roadmap

cert/map charts 272 cybersecurity certifications from 62 vendors across 16 domains and four seniority levels, from entry-level credentials such as CompTIA Security+ to expert ones such as OSCE3 and CISSP. Each entry lists the current exam price in USD, exam length and format, renewal cycle and prerequisites, checked against the vendor's own page. Pick the certifications you are aiming for to get a path ordered by prerequisite with its total cost and study time, then share it with a link.

See every certification as a plain list with prices or download the dataset.
