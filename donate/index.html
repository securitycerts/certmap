Questions

Why Zcash?

A donation to a shielded Zcash address keeps the amount and the sender private: it does not show on a public ledger the way a Bitcoin or Ethereum payment does. Neither cert/map nor anyone else can link it to you.

How do I donate?

Send any amount of ZEC from a wallet that supports shielded addresses (for example Zashi, Ywallet or Zingo) to the address on this page. On a phone, the wallet button opens your wallet with the address filled in.

What does a donation pay for?

Time: checking prices against every vendor, following retirements and new versions, testing study links and keeping the site fast and free of ads and trackers.

Does a donation change what is listed?

No. Donations are anonymous and buy nothing. Certifications are listed by the published data rules, and anyone can propose a correction on GitHub.

Other ways to help: star or contribute on GitHub, report a wrong price, or share a roadmap with someone who needs one. Questions: contact@certmap.dev.
